This version makes plenty of improvements to the
[PaigeOS 2000 project <PaigePage client:idle />](/projects/paigeos-2000), including:

- A brand-new wallpaper and startup sound!
- An enhanced Recycling Bin/Explorer window (it also acts as a very rudimentary web browser)
- Better borders\* and title bar buttons
- Custom scrollbars (note: very unfinished and a bit broken, and also Chromium only for now)
- A proper social media embed (which uses the image above)
- Some populated Start Menu items and power options
- Many behind-the-scenes changes
- And probably more things that I forgot. <small><small>Possibly even a little secret that may or
  may not be on this exact <PaigePage client:idle /> as well...</small></small>

<small>
	*: Borders do not look correct in Firefox due to [a
	bug](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=50699) that has been unresolved for **26
	years**. Sorry. Use Chromium or Safari until I get around to fixing this.
</small>
