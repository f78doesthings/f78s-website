This version marked the beginning of a new era for my website, this time with Astro and Preact. Even
back then, it was vastly superior to the
[old Jekyll site](https://github.com/f78doesthings/f78doesthings.github.io):

- Its **home page** was **expanded** some. <small>_Home Paige, if you will; I was already completely
  hooked to that game at the time..._</small>
- The **styling** was done from scratch, yet again. Now with more **fancy effects**!
- It featured several modern features provided by Astro, like **responsive images** that use modern
  formats like AVIF and adapt to your display resolution, as well as **page transitions**.
- **Preferences** made a return, allowing you to tweak the website to your liking again. _(The
  [even older pre-Jekyll site](https://github.com/f78doesthings/old-website) also had something
  similar.)_
- It even had the **animated starry background**!

And probably much more. _But I'm writing these release notes in October 2026, and this new website
released back in April, so I don't remember..._
